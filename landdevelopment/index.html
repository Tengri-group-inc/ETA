What We Actually Do

Eight core scopes. Always bundled into a plan, never sold piecemeal.

We don't bill by the hour and we don't pad invoices with mystery line items. Every scope below ladders up into a package, and every package is quoted up-front. If your job needs something we don't do, we'll tell you and point you to a sub we trust.

01

Perc & soil testing

Percolation rates, soil profile, deep-hole observation. The first answer to "can you build here?"

02

Water testing & well work

Bacteria, nitrates, pH; flow-rate and safety assessment on existing wells.

03

Septic inspection & pumping

Tank, distribution box, drain field. Standard residential pumping up to 1,000 gallons.

04

Camera & line inspection

Sewer and drain camera with recording. Hydro-jetting for blockages.

05

Filter & pump replacement

Effluent filters, risers, water pumps. Parts and labor, coordinated end-to-end.

06

Site access & clearing

Access assessment, clearing coordination, locator service for buried utilities.

07

Utility & permit coordination

Power, road, permit workflows. We hold the calendar so your team doesn't.

08

Emergency response

After-hours service for active customers — standard rates inside the PREMIUM plan.
